partial/mprotect -> PROT_NONE/R/RW/RX supported; W+X denied

partial/clock_gettime -> monotonic resolution 10 ms; realtime resolution 1 s
partial/rt_sigaction -> action layout maps; masks/delivery frame not full POSIX contract
partial/socket -> connected IPv4/IPv6 TCP/UDP with SOCK_NONBLOCK/SOCK_CLOEXEC, fcntl flag changes, read/write, poll/epoll, and exec cleanup; no listen/accept or broad socket options

abi/filesystem -> bounded flat VFS; not scalable POSIX filesystem semantics
